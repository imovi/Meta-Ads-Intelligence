# Strategy & Optimization

Use this reference after performance, competitor, and creative analysis to turn evidence into prioritized decisions.

## Decision hierarchy

1. Tracking/data quality
2. Business goal and target
3. Campaign/ad-set/ad performance
4. Creative and audience signals
5. Budget/scaling decision
6. Next test

Never optimize a campaign based on a single metric without checking the funnel and sample size.

## Health states

### Healthy

Performance meets the user's target or has strong evidence of efficiency with enough conversion volume.

Recommended behavior: preserve stability and consider gradual scaling only after checking fatigue, audience size, and trend stability.

### Needs attention

A meaningful KPI is below target, but there is enough activity to investigate.

Recommended behavior: diagnose the weakest stage before making material budget changes.

### Critical

No conversions, broken tracking, severe performance deterioration, or another material failure is evident.

Recommended behavior: investigate before adding spend.

### Watch

Data is incomplete, mixed, or not clearly above/below target.

Recommended behavior: continue collecting comparable data.

## Budget allocation

When ranking budget candidates, consider:

- spend
- conversion volume
- CPA/ROAS
- stability over time
- frequency
- creative fatigue
- audience size
- marginal performance

A high ROAS on tiny spend is not automatically a scale winner.

Use a "scale candidate" recommendation only when evidence is sufficiently strong. Otherwise use "collect more data or fix".

## Scaling

### Vertical scaling

Increase budget on a proven setup gradually and monitor downstream efficiency.

### Horizontal scaling

Expand through new creatives, audiences, placements, markets, or campaign structures where appropriate.

Do not prescribe a fixed percentage increase as a universal rule. The appropriate change depends on account history, budget size, conversion volume, and platform delivery behavior.

## Creative optimization

If CTR weakens while frequency rises, investigate creative fatigue.

If CTR is strong but conversions are weak, investigate landing page, offer, checkout, tracking, and message-to-market fit before blaming the creative.

## Competitor-informed strategy

Use competitor research to identify:

- common market angles
- overused offers
- proof patterns
- format concentration
- positioning gaps

Then create differentiated tests. Competitor observations do not prove that a competitor's strategy is profitable.

## Seven-day plan

A useful next-7-day plan should include:

- priority
- object/campaign
- current health
- action to test
- evidence
- success metric
- stop/continue condition

Keep it concrete and limited to the highest-impact actions.

## Action boundary

This reference only produces recommendations. Account changes must go through the explicit ACTION workflow and confirmation guard.

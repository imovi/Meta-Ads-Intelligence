# Client Reporting

A client report translates Meta Ads evidence into a business-readable decision document.

## Recommended format

### Executive Summary

2–5 short points covering the period's most important changes.

### KPI Snapshot

Use aggregate totals and derived metrics.

### What Worked

Highlight winners, efficient campaigns, strong creative patterns, and positive trends supported by data.

### What Needs Attention

Highlight fatigue, rising CPA, falling ROAS, tracking gaps, weak conversion volume, or other material issues.

### Recommendations

Prioritize the few actions with the strongest evidence and expected business impact.

### Next 7 Days

Turn recommendations into a practical plan with priorities.

## Source transparency

Every report should state whether the data came from:

- browser-observed Ads Manager data
- Ads Manager export
- API-shaped data
- user-provided dataset
- mock/test data

## Client-safe language

Prefer:

> “ROAS declined 18% during the period, while spend increased 12%. The decline appears concentrated in two campaigns.”

Avoid unsupported causal statements such as:

> “Facebook's algorithm caused the decline.”

## Missing data

State limitations plainly. Do not hide missing conversion, revenue, attribution, or tracking data.

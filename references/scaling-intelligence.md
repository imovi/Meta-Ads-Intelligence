# Scaling Intelligence

Use this module when deciding whether an existing campaign is ready to scale.

## Signals

Evaluate together:

- CPA vs target
- ROAS vs target
- conversion volume
- ROAS trend
- CTR trend
- frequency
- creative fatigue
- evidence quality

## Recommendations

- **Scale** — efficiency is strong, conversion evidence is meaningful, and there are no major negative trend/fatigue signals.
- **Hold** — performance is acceptable but evidence is mixed or insufficient for a material increase.
- **Reduce** — performance is materially below target with enough evidence to justify caution.
- **Test** — insufficient conversion evidence or a strong fatigue/uncertainty signal suggests experimentation first.

## Risk

Every recommendation should include risk:

- low_to_medium
- medium
- medium_to_high
- high

Risk is contextual, not a probability of failure.

## Scaling plan

When recommending scale, propose a staged change rather than assuming an unlimited increase. The exact percentage should be treated as a test parameter and adjusted for account constraints, learning phase, budget, audience size, and delivery stability.

## Important

Do not say:

> "Scale this because ROAS is high."

Prefer:

> "Scale candidate: ROAS is above target with meaningful conversion volume and no major negative trend signal. Risk: low-to-medium. Consider a staged budget increase and re-evaluate after sufficient additional data."

Scaling analysis never changes a real budget. Actual changes require the explicit Action workflow and confirmation.
